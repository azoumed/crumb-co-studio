# Home (front page)

Mode: Persuade. Scope: front page of the Crumb & Co. Studio shop in all four languages (en, ar RTL, fr, es); the header, footer, shop archive and single product inherit the same vocabulary as Operate surfaces.

Audience and job: a home baker on a phone, between batches, who knows which part of the week is eating their time. Action: pick that job, see the template that solves it, add it to the cart. Proof: the real templates themselves (columns, tabs, statuses taken from the shipped XLSX/DOCX files), shown with sample data labelled as sample. Constraints: no invented reviews, figures or guarantees; prices come from WooCommerce; WCAG AA; Core Web Vitals; logical CSS for RTL.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a triage, not a brochure. It asks one question, "what is taking your time?", and answers with the tool, working, in place. It refuses the split hero (headline left, illustration right, two buttons, badge row) and the row of four equal benefit cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cocoa #3B2A20 owns the whole first viewport as a worktop; cream type sits on it. Jobs are big cream-outlined tabs; the chosen one fills raspberry. The answer is a white spreadsheet sheet with real column heads, a tab strip and status chips in sage, raspberry cream and butter, as in the product mockups. Fraunces for the question, Nunito for the sheet, Cairo for Arabic.

STORY: The visitor recognises their week in one of four jobs, sees the exact sheet they would get, learns its format, software and price, and buys it without leaving the page. Lower down: what the whole toolkit is, how delivery works, the free tracker.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Eyebrow and Fraunces question at display size on cocoa; directly under it four job tabs (2x2 on phones, one row from 900px). Below, the active sheet panel, cropped by the fold on phones so it invites scrolling; on desktop the sheet sits beside a product strip with name, format, works-with, price and the raspberry "Add to cart, instant download" button, which is the primary action.

FORM: Task picker, position 4 of 7 on my ordered list (dealt lead), seed:7687cac6aa57dc27. Presented through the structured question tool (the user follows over Remote Control), so no decision page and code-led: no image generation was reported.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
